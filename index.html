About Me

I'm a senior Flutter developer based in Egypt, with 3.5+ years shipping production apps for Saudi companies. I own the mobile side end to end: estimate, build, store listing, then crash reports and the next slice. 20+ apps shipped across marketplaces, booking, social, and field service.

I work in feature-first Clean Architecture and BLoC/Cubit so another engineer can pick the work up. Payments, chat, maps, KYC, tracking, and in-app cameras are already in the apps I've shipped — Mada, Visa, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara, HyperPay — not a demo checkout.

I came into Flutter after QA and client work, so a ticket still has to survive a store listing — not just a pull request. I talk to the client and still write the code.

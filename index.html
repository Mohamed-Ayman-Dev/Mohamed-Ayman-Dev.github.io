About Me

I'm a Flutter developer with 3.5+ years of production experience and more than twenty shipped apps behind me, most of them for Saudi-based companies — e-commerce marketplaces, on-demand service platforms, booking systems, and a large-scale social app with real-time chat, stories, and an in-app camera.

I build with feature-first Clean Architecture: BLoC and Cubit for state, abstract repositories that keep the domain layer free of any API detail, and a typed result pattern so every network call resolves to an explicit success or failure. I've introduced that structure on client projects that started without it, and migrated live codebases over to it without pausing feature delivery.

I came into development through QA and business analysis, and that's still the part of my process teams notice. I read a requirement the way the user will actually experience it, push back on the ambiguous parts before an estimate goes out, and catch my own edge cases before anyone else opens the build.
